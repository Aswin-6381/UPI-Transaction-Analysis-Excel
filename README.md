# UPI Transaction Analysis – Dashboard



## Project Overview



This project analyzes UPI (Unified Payments Interface) transaction data using an interactive dashboard to understand transaction performance, payment behavior, transaction success rates, suspected fraud, cashback distribution, and usage patterns.



The dashboard provides insights into transaction trends, UPI applications, payment types, banks, states, and transaction activity by hour and day. The objective is to identify patterns in digital payment activity and support data-driven business decisions.



## Objectives



- Analyze total UPI transaction volume and transaction value.

- Monitor transaction success, failure, pending, and refund rates.

- Analyze transaction activity across different UPI applications.

- Identify transaction value distribution across states.

- Compare transaction amounts across banks.

- Analyze transaction types such as P2M, P2P, bill payments, and recharge.

- Identify peak transaction hours and days.

- Monitor cashback distribution and suspected fraudulent transactions.

## Key Insights



- **Transaction Volume:** The dashboard recorded 502,887 UPI transactions with a total transaction value of ₹44.25 Cr.

- **Transaction Success Rate:** Approximately 91.05% of transactions were successful, while 6.95% failed, 1.96% were pending, and 0.04% were refunded.

- **UPI App Usage:** PhonePe accounted for the largest share of transactions at 49.8%, followed by Google Pay at 21.3% and Paytm at 14.3%.

- **Transaction Type:** P2M transactions contributed the highest transaction amount at ₹18.88 Cr, followed by P2P transactions at ₹8.77 Cr.

- **Bank Performance:** HDFC Bank recorded the highest transaction amount among the banks displayed, at ₹5.89 Cr.

- **State Performance:** Maharashtra recorded the highest transaction amount among the states displayed, at ₹4.54 Cr.

- **Hourly Trends:** Transaction activity was concentrated in the evening hours, with the highest activity visible around 19:00.

- **Fraud Monitoring:** The dashboard recorded 17,089 suspected fraud transactions, highlighting the importance of monitoring and risk management.



## Business Recommendations



- **Improve Transaction Success Rate:** Investigate failed and pending transactions to identify technical issues, bank-side problems, or payment processing bottlenecks.

- **Strengthen Fraud Monitoring:** Analyze suspected fraudulent transactions to identify unusual transaction patterns and improve fraud detection mechanisms.

- **Optimize Peak-Hour Performance:** Ensure adequate system capacity and monitoring during peak transaction hours, particularly during evening periods.

- **Improve UPI App Performance:** Monitor transaction performance across payment applications and identify opportunities to improve reliability and user experience.

- **Focus on P2M Transactions:** Strengthen merchant payment services and improve the payment experience for business and retail transactions.

- **Monitor Bank Performance:** Evaluate transaction performance across banks to identify potential payment failures or service reliability issues.

- **Regional Strategy:** Use state-wise transaction patterns to identify regions with strong digital payment activity and areas where adoption could be encouraged.

- **Reduce Failed Transactions:** Analyze failure reasons and work with payment service providers and banks to improve transaction reliability.
